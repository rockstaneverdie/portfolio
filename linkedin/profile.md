## About

I'm a designer who also writes the code. I design identities, interfaces and design systems in Figma, and when a team needs a tool that doesn't exist yet, I build it: Next.js on the web, Swift on iOS.

Most of my recent work was for MIREX, an exhibition-stand builder: a token-based web design system with light and dark themes, a homepage redesign, Design Hub (an internal portal for brand files that I designed and shipped), a platform that turns sales proposals into web pages, sales decks, social campaigns and print.

On the side I build KoKo, an iOS app for learning Russian Sign Language with on-device gesture recognition (Vision, Create ML), and WhatchaSay, a Firefox extension that explains slang in videos with AI.

I use AI image models in production, with art direction and clean-up on top, and my case studies say where.
