About

I work across identity, interfaces and marketing. Most of the work here was made for MIREX, an exhibition-stand builder: a web design system, a homepage redesign, internal tools, sales decks, social campaigns and print. On the side I build KoKo, my iOS app for learning Russian Sign Language.

I like systems: tokens instead of hex codes, components instead of copies, a script instead of fifty manual exports. When a tool doesn't exist, I design it and ship it myself — mostly for the web with Next.js and TypeScript, sometimes for iOS in Swift.

I use AI image models as a production tool, with art direction and clean-up on top. Where a visual is AI-assisted, the case says so.
