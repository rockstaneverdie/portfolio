Seminar launch

An online seminar on exhibiting at medical trade shows needed a full announcement pack: an Instagram carousel, stories, Telegram squares and email banners. Each format got its own grid and safe zones, but the same typography, speaker photo and decor, so the campaign reads as one voice wherever people meet it. Texts follow the client's brief word for word.

Exhibitions of the month

A recurring carousel: one slide per trade show with dates, venue and the show's own logo, taken from its official site and cleaned up. The cover and the final slide use 3D glass objects for each industry — generated with an image model, art-directed over several rounds and composed in Figma. Two earlier cover ideas were rejected along the way.
