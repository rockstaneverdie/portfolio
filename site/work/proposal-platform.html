Proposal landing platform

A service that turns a sales proposal into a personal web page for every client — editor, landing and dashboard.

Role
Product designer
Client
MIREX
Year
2026
Stage
Spec + clickable prototype

Idea

Stand proposals were sent as heavy PDFs: no way to know if the client opened them, and every change meant a new file. The platform gives each client a personal landing page — renders of their future stand, project stages, estimate and a direct line to the manager. Client names in the screens are fictional.

For managers

Managers fill a form-based editor with a live preview next to it. The editor warns about what is still missing before publishing, and the dashboard shows every proposal with its status and views.

Status

I wrote the product spec together with the client, then built a clickable prototype in Next.js so the team could test real flows before development.
